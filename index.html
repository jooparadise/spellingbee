⌨️ Practice rules & keyboard help

Empty Enter: play the word without losing an attempt. Wrong Enter: vibration buzzer and another chance (up to 3). Correct Enter: golden bell and automatic next word after about 1.3 seconds. After 3 wrong attempts, the word's spelling appears; press Enter again to move on. Sound effects can be turned off.

First-try score: only words spelled correctly on attempt one count toward the first-try total. Any word missed on the first attempt is added to the saved review list; a later successful first attempt in a review round removes it.

Accepted spelling alternatives printed on the school list include fertiliser, materialise, odourant, glycerine, brasier, and kerosine. Capital letters and accent marks do not change scoring.
